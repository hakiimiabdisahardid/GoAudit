# Market Research: Audit & Inspection SaaS (Hiil Audit)

## 1. Overview
This report provides an analysis of the audit and inspection SaaS landscape to inform the development of Hiil Audit, a Somali-first audit/inspection platform.

## 2. Competitor Analysis: GoAudits
GoAudits is a mobile-first inspection software widely used across retail, hospitality, manufacturing, and healthcare.

### Key Features
* **Mobile-First Inspections:** Offline-first capabilities for field teams.
* **Checklist Management:** Large library of templates with some AI-assisted generation.
* **Reporting:** Instant report generation and automated distribution.
* **Corrective Actions:** Built-in workflows for addressing inspection failures.

### Pricing Model
| Plan | Cost (USD) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Starter | ~$10 - $12/user/mo | Limited templates and locations. |
| Enterprise | ~$30 - $35/user/mo | Unlimited features and access. |

*Note: Pricing is typically billed annually and compounds quickly for large franchise networks.*

## 3. Market Insights & Opportunities
### Common Pain Points in Existing Solutions
* **Pricing Friction:** Per-user pricing models become prohibitively expensive for large, multi-location franchise networks.
* **Operational Silos:** Inspection findings often stay "siloed" and do not integrate directly with SOPs or training/LMS systems.
* **Lack of Hierarchical Models:** Many general-purpose tools lack specific permission structures for multi-tier organizations (e.g., Franchisor vs. Franchisee vs. Area Manager).
* **Usability Constraints:** Issues with mobile app stability during photo uploads or limited batching capabilities can slow down field work.

## 5. Recommended Strategic Directions for Hiil Audit
1. **Somali-First Localization:** Focus on native language support and localized business workflows which are often ignored by global incumbents.
2. **Tiered Pricing:** Consider "per-location" or "flat-fee" pricing models to better serve the local market economics, avoiding the "per-user" penalty for large teams.
3. **Integrated SOPs/Training:** Build a "closed-loop" system where audit failures trigger immediate, automated assignment of corrective training or standard operating procedures (SOPs).
4. **Role-Based Hierarchy:** Implement a robust hierarchy from the start to cater to multi-location businesses, allowing for distinct views for owners, managers, and auditors.
